- [x] References [1] through [14] are all cited in the manuscript; no cited number is missing from the reference list.

## Financial planning

IEEE Access currently states an article processing charge of USD 2,160 plus applicable taxes for an accepted article. The fee is charged after acceptance, not at initial submission. Before submitting, check IEEE membership discounts and whether a university or institutional library has an IEEE open-access agreement or deposit account. No waiver or discount should be assumed until IEEE confirms it.
