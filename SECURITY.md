# Security Policy

## Research status

This repository contains an unaudited research prototype. Do not use it to custody funds, issue a production asset, protect safety-critical systems, or make compliance claims.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report suspected vulnerabilities privately to `murukutlassl@gmail.com`. Include the affected component, reproduction steps, impact, and any suggested mitigation. Do not include real credentials, personal data, or exploit activity against systems you do not own or have explicit permission to test.

## Disclosure expectations

The maintainer will acknowledge a complete report, investigate it, and coordinate a responsible disclosure timeline appropriate to the risk. No bounty is promised.
